Product Details

The technology and craft behind every NEXVOLT product

Premium Materials

Every NEXVOLT can is precision‑drawn from food‑grade aluminium alloy, with a polymer inner lining that preserves the formula and a high‑definition lithographic print applied at 600 dpi.

Signature Design

The slim 250 ml silhouette was purpose‑built for one‑handed grip. The angular shoulder taper and embossed NEXVOLT wordmark are registered design elements, recognisable at a glance.

Real‑Time 3D

Powered by THREE.js and WebGL, this viewer renders PBR materials with image‑based lighting, a custom GLSL rim‑glow shader, ACES filmic tone mapping, and Unreal-Engine-style bloom post‑processing — entirely in the browser.
